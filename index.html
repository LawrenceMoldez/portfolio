Projects

Selected work

RiverWatch

CapstoneIoTMobile app

An Internet of Things-based river water-level monitoring system with multi-level flood alerts for Barangay Bulacao, Cebu City. A water-level sensor connected to a microcontroller sends readings to a mobile app, which sorts them into four alert levels: Normal, Alert, Warning, and Critical. Barangay officials log in to monitor the river, and residents can view the readings and alert status without logging in.

Type
Capstone project
Team
Five members
My role
Programmer on the capstone team, working on the system's software. I also helped with project documentation, including the storyboard and the transmittal letter for interviews

Something Is Different

Roblox gameHorror and explorationIn development

A Backrooms-inspired horror and exploration game for Roblox, made for our game development class. Players wake up in an endless, empty space and must explore, manage hunger, thirst, and stamina, and look for a way out. The game focuses on atmosphere and survival instead of combat.

  • Hunger, thirst, and stamina systems
  • Sprinting and a flashlight
  • First-person camera and limited movement to keep the experience isolating
Type
Game development class project
Team
Four members: Lawrence Moldez (programmer), Bia Carmel Heruela (builder), Robie Soria (UI and quality testing), and Joy Loren Muaña (game designer)
Tools
Roblox Studio, Luau
My role
Programmer: I developed most of the game, including the scripts for hunger and thirst, sprinting and stamina, the flashlight, and the first-person and movement restrictions
